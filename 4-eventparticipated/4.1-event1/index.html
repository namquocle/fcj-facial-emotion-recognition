Event 1

SUMMARY REPORT: “STUDY TOUR: AWS — ENTERPRISE CLOUD ARCHITECTURES & INDUSTRY APPLICATIONS”

Event Objectives

  • Update industry insights on Cloud Computing, Generative AI (GenAI), and hiring trends.
  • Bridge the gap between academic knowledge and real-world enterprise data/cloud architectures.
  • Provide career guidance on transitioning from a student to a full-fledged Data/Cloud Engineer.
  • Facilitate networking between students and industry experts across the AWS ecosystem.

Event Details & Speakers

  • Organizers & Partners: Swinburne Vietnam, AWS Vietnam, Renova Cloud, Cloud Kinetics.
  • Participants: Solution Architects, GenAI Engineers, Data Engineers, and over 100 Swinburne Vietnam students.

Key Highlights

1. Overview of Cloud & GenAI Job Market Trends

  • Industry shift: Combining flexible Cloud infrastructure with GenAI capabilities is reshaping how enterprises manage large-scale data systems.
  • Hiring demand: Companies prioritize candidates with practical hands-on experience, strong Cloud-native skills, and the ability to apply GenAI in real-world scenarios.

2. Transitioning from Campus to Real-World Practice

  • Career transformation: Real-world lessons on evolving from a university student into a professional Data Engineer.
  • Data architecture optimization: How enterprises build, manage, and optimize large-scale data architectures in production environments.

3. Bridging the Skills Gap (Learning by Doing)

  • Turning theoretical knowledge into practical solutions to stand out to employers.
  • Enhancing practical skills through hands-on labs and industry certifications to validate competencies.

4. Fireside Chat & Industry Panel

  • Strategies for nurturing and developing the next generation of tech talent in Vietnam.
  • Cultivating a strong problem-solving mindset and a habit of continuous self-learning.

Key Takeaways

Design Mindset & Career Orientation

  • Action-oriented mindset: Technology evolves constantly, but problem-solving capability and proactive hands-on experience remain the key drivers of success.
  • Business-first approach: Always start with real-world business challenges before deciding on the technical stack.

Technical & Professional Insights

  • Cloud & AI integration: Understanding how Cloud and GenAI complement each other to optimize workflows and data processing.
  • Career readiness: Building a personal portfolio with practical projects and earning recognized industry certifications.

Applying to Work & Study

  • Skill Roadmap: Focus on developing core skills in Cloud Architecture and Data Engineering.
  • Hands-on Projects: Apply Cloud/GenAI solutions to course assignments, capstone projects, or internship tasks.
  • Community Engagement: Actively participate in technical communities (e.g., AWS Study Group) for continuous learning and networking opportunities.

Event Experience

Attending the Study Tour: AWS — Enterprise Cloud Architectures & Industry Applications at Bitexco Financial Tower was an invaluable experience. Key highlights included:

  • Direct interaction with industry experts: Gained practical insights from AWS, Renova Cloud, and Cloud Kinetics specialists through interactive Q&A sessions.
  • Real-world case studies: Understood how modern architectures are deployed in large-scale enterprise environments.
  • Networking opportunities: Connected with peers, mentors, and industry professionals to expand career perspectives.
