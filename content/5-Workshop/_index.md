4. **5.4 Steps / UI & API:** [Building the Streamlit frontend and integrating S3 uploads](5.4-UI-API/)
5. **5.5 Testing / Policy:** [Executing end-to-end testing and verifying logs & databases](5.5-Testing-Policy/)
6. **5.6 Cleanup:** [Destroying AWS resources and stopping local servers to avoid costs](5.6-Cleanup/)
