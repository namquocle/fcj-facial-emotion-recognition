Below are my personal thoughts, feedback, and suggestions after completing the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** internship program. These evaluations are shared objectively and honestly based on my learning journey and the development of my hands-on project.

---

## I. General Program Assessment

### 1. Learning and Working Environment
- The learning and working environment is highly comfortable, open, and flexible. The program provides maximum support for students to self-study, perform lab exercises, and freely experiment with new technological solutions on AWS.

### 2. Support from Mentors and Program Admins
- The mentors are extremely supportive, attentive, and highly skilled. Whenever I encountered complex issues during project implementation (such as IAM permission policies, Boto3 client issues on Lambda, or data conversion bugs in DynamoDB), I received immediate guidance and support to resolve those bottlenecks.

### 3. Career and Academic Major Alignment
- The program aligns perfectly with my Information Technology major at Sai Gon University. The Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence (AI/ML) topics are highly relevant to current industry demands, helping to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world system deployments.

### 4. Opportunities for Learning and Skill Development
- Learning opportunities are abundant. Beyond executing hands-on labs in a standard AWS environment, I also developed systems-design thinking for Serverless applications, event-driven programming, and built the habit of producing structured, professional documentation in both English and Vietnamese.

### 5. Culture and Team Collaboration
- The learning culture among student peers is active and positive. Students are always ready to assist and exchange troubleshooting experiences on the public discussion channels, which provided great motivation to complete the project.

### 6. Program Policies and Benefits
- The program organizers prepared educational resources thoroughly (e.g., AWS Academy Learner Labs), providing a well-structured roadmap and intuitive documentation, allowing students to focus fully on research and building their products.

---

## II. Survey Questions

### 1. What satisfied you the most when participating in the program?
- I am most satisfied with being able to build my own project, **"Serverless Facial Emotion Recognition Analytics Platform"**, and successfully deploying an End-to-End automated pipeline on a real AWS Serverless architecture. Watching an uploaded image trigger Lambda, Rekognition, and log emotion metrics into DynamoDB was a highly rewarding experience.

### 2. What do you think should be improved or changed in the program?
- I hope the program will organize more interactive team-bonding sessions or informal knowledge-sharing events to increase engagement among members. Additionally, adding short technical workshops on CI/CD pipelines (for automated deployment) and deep dives into Cost Optimization would make the student projects closer to production-ready standards.

### 3. Would you recommend this program to your friends or future student groups? Why?
- **Definitely yes.** This is an exceptionally high-quality program for anyone wanting to build skills in Cloud and AI. The practical curriculum, experienced mentors, and hands-on AWS console environment help students accelerate their capability in a short timeframe.

---

## III. Future Suggestions and Desires

- **Upgrade Suggestions:** I recommend adding guides for Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools like Terraform or AWS SAM to help students learn infrastructure automation.
- **Future Goals:** I am highly motivated and look forward to participating in future stages of the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) or other programs organized by AWS.
